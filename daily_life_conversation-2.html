English Speaking Practice · 2 People

Daily Life

A daily conversation between two friends — from morning routines to school, free time, and evening habits.

01

Greeting and Morning Routine

Starting the day
Person A
Good morning! How are you today?
Person B
Good morning! I'm fine, thank you. How about you?
Person A
I'm good, too. What time did you wake up this morning?
Person B
I woke up at six o'clock. What about you?
Person A
I woke up at half past six because I was tired last night.
Person B
Did you go to bed late last night?
Person A
Yes, I did. I finished my homework at about eleven o'clock.
Person B
Oh, I see. What do you usually do after waking up?
Person A
I brush my teeth, wash my face, and take a shower. Then I get dressed.
Person B
That's good! Do you eat breakfast every morning?
Person A
Yes, I usually eat rice or bread and drink some milk. What about you?
Person B
I usually eat noodles because they are quick and delicious.
02

School Life

Classes, subjects, and friends
Person A
What time do you go to school?
Person B
I usually leave home at seven o'clock. How do you go to school?
Person A
I go to school by motorcycle with my family. Sometimes I go with my friends.
Person B
What is your favorite subject?
Person A
My favorite subject is English because I want to speak English fluently.
Person B
That's great! Is English difficult for you?
Person A
Sometimes it is difficult, especially grammar and pronunciation. But I keep practicing.
Person B
That's a good attitude. What do you usually do during the break?
Person A
I usually talk with my friends, eat snacks, and sometimes play games. What about you?
Person B
I like talking with my classmates. Sometimes we talk about movies, music, and our future plans.
Person A
That sounds interesting! Do you have a lot of homework?
Person B
Yes, especially when we have mathematics and chemistry homework.
Person A
I understand. We should help each other when we don't understand something.
Person B
You're right! Studying together makes learning easier.
03

Afternoon and Free Time

Hobbies and future plans
Person A
What time do your classes finish?
Person B
My classes usually finish at four o'clock in the afternoon.
Person A
What do you do after school?
Person B
I go home, take a shower, and have something to eat. Then I relax for a while.
Person A
Do you like playing video games?
Person B
Yes, I do. I usually play games with my friends when I have free time. Do you play games, too?
Person A
Yes, but I also enjoy editing videos and learning new things on the internet.
Person B
Really? That sounds cool! What kind of videos do you edit?
Person A
I like editing short videos with music, effects, and transitions.
Person B
That's awesome! I want to learn video editing, too. Can you teach me someday?
Person A
Of course! We can learn together after finishing our homework.
Person B
Great! By the way, what do you want to be in the future?
Person A
I want to work with computers and technology because I enjoy learning how things work.
Person B
That's a good goal. I hope you can achieve your dream.
Person A
Thank you! I hope you achieve your dream, too.
04

Evening and Night Routine

Dinner, studying, and saying goodbye
Person B
What time do you usually have dinner?
Person A
I usually have dinner with my family at seven o'clock. What about you?
Person B
I usually eat dinner at the same time. After dinner, I help my family clean the house.
Person A
That's nice. Do you study again at night?
Person B
Yes, I review my lessons and finish my homework. Sometimes I watch English videos to improve my listening.
Person A
That's a good idea. I also want to improve my English speaking skills.
Person B
We can practice speaking English together every day.
Person A
That's a great idea! We can talk about our daily lives for fifteen minutes every day.
Person B
Sure! What time do you usually go to bed?
Person A
I usually go to bed at ten o'clock, but sometimes I stay up late.
Person B
You should get enough sleep so that you have energy for the next day.
Person A
You're right. I'll try to sleep earlier and manage my time better.
Person B
That's good! I think having a daily routine helps us become more responsible.
Person A
I agree with you. Anyway, it's getting late. I have to go now.
Person B
All right! See you at school tomorrow. Have a good night!
Person A
See you tomorrow! Good night!
